Food

Eating isn’t just counting calories.

Log your meals and understand your habits. Type what you ate, scan a barcode, or save the dishes you eat often.

  1. Take a photoPoint at your plate and VitaLinks prepares an estimate for you to review before saving.
  2. VitaLinks estimatesCalories and macronutrients, quickly and simply.
  3. Understand your plateSee what each meal adds to your day. You review and adjust before saving.

Calories from a photo are included in VitaLinks Plus, in the mobile app. Values are AI-generated estimates, not medical advice.

Movement

Moving counts too.

It doesn’t have to be intense. It just has to be yours. Log a walk, a yoga class or a weights session.

In the mobile app, VitaLinks brings in your steps and workouts from Health Connect or Apple Health.
