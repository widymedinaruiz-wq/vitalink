Delete Your Account & Data

Applies to the VitaLinks web app and its Android app

The short version: if you've never signed in, your data only ever existed on your own device — uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes everything, with nothing further to request. If you signed in with Google to sync across devices, email us to request deletion of that synced copy.

How to request deletion

  1. Email widymedinaruiz@gmail.com from the address associated with your Google sign-in, with the subject "Delete my VitaLinks account" (or use the button below).
  2. We'll confirm your identity against the sign-in email and process the request.
  3. You'll get a confirmation reply once your account and synced data have been permanently deleted — typically within a few business days.

Email us to delete your account

What gets deleted

  • Your Firebase account (the sign-in itself).
  • Your synced cloud data — meals, exercise, blood pressure, medications, body composition, weight, sleep, water, and any Health Connect data that was synced.
  • Your VitaLinks Plus subscription status record, if applicable (this does not cancel an active Play Store subscription — cancel that separately from Google Play's own subscription management, the same place you subscribed).

What isn't affected

  • Data stored only on your own device (if you never signed in, or on a device you don't sync) is never something we hold — deleting it is done locally, in the app, or by uninstalling.
  • Anonymized nutrition facts you may have contributed to the shared food database (calories, protein, etc. for a food item) are kept, since they're never linked to your identity or account in the first place.

Retention period

Deletion is processed promptly once we confirm your request — normally within a few business days, and never longer than 30 days.
